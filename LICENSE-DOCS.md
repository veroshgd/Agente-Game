## Note on the preregistration texts

`docs/MEMORY_TRANSFER029_PREREGISTRATION.md` and the other preregistration
files are released so that the confirmatory claims in the paper can be checked
against what was actually fixed in advance. They are historical records. You
are free to redistribute and adapt them under CC BY 4.0, but an adapted
preregistration is no longer evidence about *this* study, and its SHA-256 will
no longer match the value frozen in `final_029.py`. Please make any such
adaptation clearly distinguishable from the original.
